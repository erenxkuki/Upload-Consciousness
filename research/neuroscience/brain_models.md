# Brain-Inspired Models (research)

## Scope

Investigate increasingly sophisticated cognitive architectures and brain-inspired
models. No claim of actual brain emulation or consciousness transfer.

## Areas of investigation

- Connectomics and structural brain mapping (reference only).
- Neural population dynamics.
- Memory consolidation (hippocampal-cortical transfer) as inspiration for
  memory consolidation proposals.
- Emotional regulation circuits as inspiration for endocrine-like regulation.
- Attention and salience networks.
- Self-model and metacognition.

## Computational experiments (planned)

- Compare memory consolidation heuristics against human-like forgetting curves.
- Test self-model stability under perturbation.
- Measure identity reconstruction accuracy after checkpoint/restore.
- Evaluate embodied interaction effects on self-model coherence.

## Constraints

- Do not fabricate neuroscience references.
- Distinguish metaphor from mechanism.
- Document limitations of every model.
- Never claim brain emulation.
