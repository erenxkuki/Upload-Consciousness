# Feasibility Assessment

## Overall verdict

The vision splits into three feasibility classes.

### Implementable today (engineering)

- Versioned identity records with provenance and integrity verification.
- Multi-dimensional emotional state with explainable transitions.
- Personality as stable/adaptable parameter sets with bounded drift.
- Cognitive orchestration over replaceable inference providers.
- Episodic/semantic memory with correction history and provenance.
- Distributed continuity: checkpoints, migration, recovery, conflict detection.
- Consent, lifecycle, privacy, audit logging.
- Digital embodiment and multi-entity virtual worlds.

These are well-understood software engineering problems. The repository now contains
working foundations for all of them (Phases 1-7).

### Research problems (not yet solvable as product)

- Faithful whole-brain emulation.
- Substrate-independent consciousness.
- Reliable reconstruction of subjective experience from behavioural data.
- Validated measures of psychological continuity across substrates.
- Emotion model validity vs subjective experience.

### Currently impossible to verify

- That a digital system is conscious.
- That continuity of data equals continuity of a person.
- That an uploaded mind "is" the original person.
- Immortality or endless desirable existence.

## Component-by-component feasibility

| Component | Feasibility | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Identity representation | High | Persistent versioned records; standard identity management. |
| Memory provenance | High | Event sourcing + provenance chains. |
| Personality modelling | Medium-High | Psychometric trait models; drift bounded and explainable. |
| Emotional modelling | Medium | Dimensional models (valence/arousal/etc.) are standard; subjective experience not captured. |
| Cognitive orchestration | Medium | Depends on inference provider quality; identity preserved independently. |
| Distributed continuity | Medium | Strong-consistency authority is a bottleneck but feasible. |
| Embodiment | Medium | Avatar control well-understood; sensory fidelity limited. |
| Virtual worlds | Medium-High | Standard multiplayer simulation. |
| Consciousness transfer | Not feasible | Open research question; no validation method. |

## Key risks

1. Split-brain identity under network partition (mitigated by centralized authority).
2. Memory provenance erosion across transformations (mitigated by provenance chains).
3. Over-claiming consciousness from behaviour (mitigated by scientific boundaries doc).
4. Forced digital existence (mitigated by autonomy policy).
5. Backup ghosts after erasure (documented limitation).

## Recommended next research steps

- Formalize "one coherent identity history" as an invariant with tests.
- Develop measurable psychological-continuity evaluation protocols.
- Investigate embodied interaction effects on self-model coherence.
- Evaluate brain-inspired architectures (spiking networks, predictive processing) as
  optional cognitive backends, without consciousness claims.
