# Consciousness Research Module

## Purpose

Scientific investigation, not declaration of consciousness achieved.

## Theories under investigation

For each: central claims, scientific support, criticisms, unresolved questions,
computational implications, and the distinction between theoretical compatibility
and empirical proof.

- Global Neuronal Workspace Theory (GNWT)
- Integrated Information Theory (IIT)
- Higher-Order Theories (HOT)
- Predictive Processing
- Recurrent Processing Theory
- Embodied Cognition
- Cognitive Neuroscience
- Whole-Brain Emulation research

## Experiments (planned)

- Memory continuity.
- Psychological consistency.
- Self-model stability.
- Cognitive adaptation.
- Temporal continuity.
- Embodied interaction.
- Identity reconstruction accuracy.

## Rules

- Never label a behavioural benchmark as a consciousness detector.
- Never claim consciousness based on ability to discuss consciousness.
- Distinguish theoretical compatibility from empirical proof.
- No fabricated references.
