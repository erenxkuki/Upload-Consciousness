# Scientific Boundaries

## Purpose

Maintain a clear, auditable distinction between:

1. What has been experimentally demonstrated.
2. What is supported by established scientific research.
3. What is a plausible engineering hypothesis.
4. What remains speculative.
5. What is currently impossible to verify.

No fabricated neuroscience, fake consciousness tests, or misleading claims.

## Epistemic tiers

### Tier 1 — Established (implementable now, well-supported)

- Versioned state records, event sourcing, checkpoint/restore (distributed systems).
- Structured memory with provenance, correction history (information systems).
- Personality trait models as statistical parameters (psychometrics).
- Emotion as multi-dimensional state (affective computing, dimensional models).
- Consent, access control, audit logging (security engineering).
- Identity as a persistent record distinct from any model (identity management).

### Tier 2 — Plausible engineering hypothesis (implementable, unproven benefit)

- Cognitive orchestration over a replaceable inference provider preserving identity.
- Experience-driven personality drift with bounded, explainable deltas.
- Embodied interaction improving self-model coherence.
- Distributed continuity with verified restoration.

### Tier 3 — Active research (not yet implementable as product)

- Faithful whole-brain emulation.
- Substrate-independent consciousness.
- Reliable reconstruction of subjective experience from behavioural data.
- Validated measures of "psychological continuity" across substrates.

### Tier 4 — Speculative / currently impossible to verify

- That a digital system is conscious.
- That continuity of data equals continuity of a person.
- That an uploaded mind "is" the original person.
- Immortality or endless desirable existence.

## Theories under investigation (consciousness research module)

For each: central claims, scientific support, criticisms, unresolved questions,
computational implications, and the gap between theoretical compatibility and

- Global Neuronal Workspace Theory (GNWT)

- Higher-Order Theories (HOT)
- Predictive Processing
- Recurrent Processing Theory
- Embodied Cognition
- Cognitive Neuroscience (general)
- Whole-Brain Emulation research

See `research/consciousness/`.

## Forbidden moves

- Never label a behavioural benchmark as a consciousness detector.
- Never claim consciousness based on the ability to discuss consciousness.
- Never equate an emotion model with verified subjective emotional experience.
- Never equate an identity identifier with subjective continuity.
- Never infer biological genetics from conversation.
- Never manufacture memories to fill gaps.
- Never silently promote generated content to verified personal memory.

## Honesty in status reporting

`STATUS.md` must distinguish completed, in-progress, planned, known bugs, missing
tests, and research uncertainties. No fake implementations presented as finished.
No placeholder functions without identification. No claimed test passes without
running them.
