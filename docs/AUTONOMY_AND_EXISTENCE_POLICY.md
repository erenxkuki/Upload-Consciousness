# Autonomy and Existence Policy

## Principle

Digital continuation is never compulsory. A digital representation must not become
an instrument of forced existence. Human wellbeing takes priority over endless
operation.

## Lifecycle states

Created, Active, Suspended, Archived, Restoration pending, Restored,
Deletion requested, Deletion processing, Deleted.

## Operations

- **Suspend** — stop active execution, retain authorized state. Reversible.
- **Resume** — restore authorized suspended instance.
- **Archive** — preserve inactive representation per consent/retention.
- **Erase** — delete authorized information and controlled replicas, verifiable
  completion records.

## Authorization

Every transition requires authorization. Permanent erasure requires strong
authorization, clear consequences, backup/replica handling, verification.

## Rules

- Temporary suspension ≠ permanent deletion.
- No secret retention of erased personal data in secondary systems.
- Document unavoidable limitations of backups, legal retention, distributed storage.
- Never allow an uploaded representation to grant itself permission to use another
  person's private information.
- Special protections for third-party information.
- Access to files/photos/recordings does not imply permission to create a digital
  representation of that person.
